Copy & Send

Hotel Messages

Two short messages that prevent the two most likely problems: a locked door at 23:00 and a missing airport car on the last night.

Message 1 — Late check-in (Airport Homestay)

📤 When to send: right after booking the homestay (any channel — Agoda/Booking message or WhatsApp).

Hello, my name is [YOUR NAME] and I have a booking for Wednesday 29 July 2026 (booking reference: [REF]). My flight EK370 lands at Da Nang Airport at 21:50, so I expect to arrive at the homestay around 22:45–23:00. Could you please confirm that late check-in is OK and that my room will be kept? If reception is closed at that hour, please let me know how to enter (door code, key box, or a phone number to call). Thank you very much!

Message 2 — Airport car (BlueSun Danang Beach Hotel)

📤 When to send: 2–3 days before checkout (so roughly Day 3–4 of the trip).

Hello, my name is [YOUR NAME], booking reference [REF], staying 1–3 August 2026. My departure flight EK371 leaves Da Nang at 23:30 on Monday 3 August, and I would like to arrive at the airport by 21:00. I understand the hotel can arrange an airport car — could you please reserve a car to pick me up at the hotel at 20:15 on 3 August? Kindly confirm whether it is complimentary or the price, and where I should meet the driver. Thank you very much!

⚠️ BlueSun transfer caveat

Public sources list BlueSun's airport transfer as a PAID service ~VND 300,000/vehicle, running 06:00–22:00 — the traveler was told it is free, so the message politely asks the hotel to confirm. If it's paid or unavailable: Grab fallback 100–150k, request by 19:50.

Practical Guide

Travel Tips

  • Grab: pay cash in VND small notes; Be / Xanh SM apps are 10–20% cheaper at surge.
  • Early August weather: Da Nang 30–35°C with brief showers; Ba Na summit is 8–12°C cooler — pack a light jacket + raincoat.
  • Pack: swimwear (beach + VinWonders water park), grippy shoes (Marble Mountains), modest clothes (pagodas), sunscreen — and leave EMPTY SPACE in your luggage for souvenirs.
  • Money rules: seafood — ALWAYS agree the per-kg price + watch the weighing; basket boat tip 50–100k is normal.
  • Markets: bargain 20–30% at Han/Con Market; Lotte Mart is fixed prices with sealed, flight-safe packs.
  • Connectivity: eSIM before departure (~$8) or the Viettel counter at DAD arrivals.
