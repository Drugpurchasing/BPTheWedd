Choosing between them

Lean Evenity when…

  • Rapid BMD increase — especially hip — is the priority
  • Monthly clinic visits are acceptable
  • No recent myocardial infarction or stroke
  • A 12-month anabolic burst fits the care plan

Lean Forteo when…

  • Daily self-injection is manageable
  • Cardiovascular risk limits Evenity use
  • Treatment may need up to 24 months
  • Broader populations (e.g. men, glucocorticoid-related)
