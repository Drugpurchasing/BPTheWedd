Choosing between them

Lean Evenity when…

  • Rapid BMD increase (especially hip) is the priority
  • Monthly clinic visits are acceptable
  • No recent MI or stroke
  • A 12-month anabolic burst fits the plan

Lean Forteo when…

  • Daily self-injection is manageable
  • Cardiovascular risk limits Evenity
  • Treatment may need up to 24 months
  • Broader labeled populations (e.g., men, glucocorticoid-related)
