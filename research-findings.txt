- **Location**: Cam Thanh commune, ~3–5 km southeast of Hoi An Old Town (the classic "Rừng Dừa Bảy Mẫu" / Bay Mau coconut forest area along Trần Nhân Tông street). ~15 min by Grab (~80,000–100,000 VND) or a pleasant 30-min bicycle ride. [Asia Tour Advisor](https://www.asiatouradvisor.com/get-inspired/vietnam/cam-thanh-coconut-village/); [Namia River Retreat, Mar 2026](https://namiariverretreat.com/tra-que-vegetable-village-and-cam-thanh-village/)
- **Costs (2025–2026)**:
  - Village entrance/eco-fee: ~30,000–50,000 VND/person (mandatory, collected at village gates). [Vietnam Explore, May 2026](https://vietnamexplore.vn/en/cam-thanh-coconut-village-ticket-price/)
  - Basket boat: ~280,000 VND per boat (2 adults + 1 child under 1 m); smaller boats ~150,000 VND. Per-person quotes run 150,000–250,000 VND if booking on the spot. [Vietnam Airlines travel guide, Jul 2026](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/cn/en/plan-book/travel/travel-guide/coconut-village-hoi-an); [Làng Dừa Xanh, Jul 2026](https://langduaxanh.com/blog/coconut-forest-basket-boat-ride-price/)
- **Duration/hours**: ride 45–60 min; operators roughly 08:00–17:30 daily, no weekday closures. [Traveloka, 2025](https://www.traveloka.com/en-id/activities/vietnam/product/coconut-jungle-basket-boat-ride-tickets-8127488081576)
- **Walk-in vs booking**: walk-in is fine and cheapest if you go directly to a named operator's pier (avoid roadside touts who sell "cheap" tickets that become short rides + hidden fees). Klook/GetYourGuide bundles cost more but fix the price.
- **Tourist-trap honesty**: the mid-river "basket boat spinning show," karaoke-speaker boats, and aggressive tip demands are real — expect a 50,000–100,000 VND tip ask for spinning/photos. It's kitschy but most travelers rate it fun if you embrace it; opt out of spinning when boarding if prone to motion sickness.
- **Best-reviewed operators**: Hạnh Coconut (Hanh Coconut — VinWonders even bundles it in their combo ticket), Làng Dừa Xanh (Green Coconut Village), and long-standing "Hoi An Basket Boat Tours" (TripAdvisor-listed). Book direct at pier or via hotel.
